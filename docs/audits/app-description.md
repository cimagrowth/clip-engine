## Short description (OAuth / TikTok “what your app does”, ~1–2 sentences)

Clip Engine is a web app that helps creators review short clips they made from source videos they have the right to use, edit the title and caption, choose privacy and audience settings, and publish those clips to their own YouTube and TikTok accounts.

## Longer description (YouTube audit “API Client” / website homepage)

Clip Engine is a creator publishing tool. A signed-in creator:

1. Reviews a queued short-form clip next to the rules that apply to that clip.
2. Edits the title, description, and other metadata. Suggested text is always editable. Nothing is appended after the creator submits.
3. Chooses visibility (and, on TikTok, interaction and commercial-content settings) with no hidden defaults that override the creator.
4. Explicitly approves the post. Clip Engine then uploads or publishes only to the YouTube channel or TikTok profile the creator connected.
5. Sees the resulting post URL and status. The creator can disconnect the account at any time.

Clip Engine does not buy views or engagement, does not post without that approval, and does not upload material the creator does not have rights to use. It is not a child-directed app.
